Conclusion

Conclusion

A simple linear regression model was developed to evaluate the relationship between bill length (mm) and body mass (g) for Adelie and Gentoo penguins.

The model produced an R² value of 0.769, indicating that approximately 76.9% of the variation in penguin body mass can be explained by bill length alone. This suggests a strong linear relationship between the two variables.

The regression coefficient for bill length (141.19) indicates that, on average, for every 1 mm increase in bill length, body mass is predicted to increase by approximately 141 grams.

The predictor was found to be statistically significant (p < 0.001), providing evidence that bill length contributes meaningfully to predicting body mass.

Residual diagnostics further suggest that the regression assumptions are reasonably satisfied: - Residuals appear approximately normally distributed - No major systematic patterns were observed - The model demonstrates acceptable fit for simple linear regression

Overall, the analysis indicates that bill length is a strong predictor of penguin body mass within the selected species group.
